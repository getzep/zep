CAPA record R03, dated 2026-03-05, prepared by Priya Raman.

CAPA-118 was opened for quality issue QI-1041, the Aster 410 flow-rate
under-delivery. I am the CAPA owner.

The suspected root cause is calibration drift in FS-20 flow sensors, specifically
Vossbridge Sensors lot VS-2611. As a containment action, lot VS-2611 was quarantined
at the Galway plant and blocked from production use.

Nadia Haddad will review the incoming inspection process at Vossbridge Sensors to
determine how drifted sensors passed acceptance testing. Corrective action options
include a firmware compensation table and a revised sensor design.
