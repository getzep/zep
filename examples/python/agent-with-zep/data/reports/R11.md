Regulatory update R11, dated 2026-06-30, prepared by Ines Carvalho.

The Fernway EU MDR CE certificate expired on 2026-06-30. The renewal application
(submitted on 2026-04-22) is still under review by the notified body, Lindqvist
Certification.

EU sales of Fernway stopped on 2026-06-30 and will remain stopped until the
renewal is issued. Fernway remains on the market in the United States under its
cleared 510(k). Regulatory Affairs will track the renewal review and report the
decision date when the notified body provides it.
