CAPA record R13, dated 2026-07-09, prepared by Priya Raman.

CAPA-118 update: the verification of effectiveness for firmware 2.3.1 FAILED. The
assessment was based on the St. Brigid's Hospital field service report (R10), which
showed under-delivery recurring on updated pumps in cool rooms.

CAPA-118 and QI-1041 remain open.

The new corrective action is to replace the FS-20 with the revised FS-20B flow
sensor from Vossbridge Sensors. FS-20B is in qualification and is not yet in
production. Target completion is 2026-10-31. Reliability testing is assigned to
Lena Vogt and Owen Briggs.
