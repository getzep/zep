Engineering test report R14, dated 2026-07-22, prepared by Aiko Tanaka.

Verification testing of the Lyric 350 found that in 3% of 400 test runs, alarm
forwarding to the central station through the WM-4 wireless module dropped for more
than 10 seconds.

I opened QI-1068 with severity high because a missed alarm is a patient-safety
issue in the pre-market phase. The issue owner is Tomás Ibarra. The issue blocks
launch until a fix is verified. Further root-cause work on the WM-4 module is in
progress with Tamsin Electronics.
