Sales note R06, dated 2026-05-02, prepared by Erik Sandoval.

I met with the clinical engineering team at Northgate Regional Hospital. I told
them that "the Aster 410 flow issue is resolved with firmware 2.3.1".

Northgate agreed to resume using the pumps on the ward. No verification evidence
was cited in the discussion; the statement was based on the engineering release
notice for firmware 2.3.1. I asked the hospital to report any further flow-rate
discrepancies through the complaint channel.
