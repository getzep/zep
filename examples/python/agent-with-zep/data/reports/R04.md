CAPA record R04, dated 2026-03-27, prepared by Lena Vogt.

CAPA-109 for QI-1033, the Lyric 300 display flicker, is closed. Tamsin Electronics
changed the DP-12 display connector, and the revised connector resolved the flicker
root cause.

Verification of effectiveness: we reviewed 90 days of field data from 140 units
with the new connector and recorded zero flicker reports. The verification criteria
were met. Status: closed, verified. No further action is required on QI-1033.
