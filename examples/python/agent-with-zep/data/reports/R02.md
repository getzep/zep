Field service report R02, dated 2026-02-20, prepared by Colm Brennan.

I inspected two returned Aster 410 pumps from Northgate Regional Hospital. On the
test bench, both units under-delivered by about 8% at a programmed rate of 100 mL/h.
The delivery error was consistent across repeated runs.

Both units contain FS-20 flow sensors from Vossbridge Sensors lot VS-2611. The
sensor serial numbers were recorded and passed to Quality for comparison against
the complaint units still in the field. No mechanical damage was found on either
unit. The pumps were held at the Galway plant for further analysis.
