Regulatory update R07, dated 2026-05-12, prepared by Julien Moreau.

The 510(k) premarket notification for the Lyric 350 patient monitor was submitted
to the FDA on 2026-05-12. The submission covers the next-generation monitor that
succeeds the Lyric 300.

The review clock starts from the submission date. Regulatory Affairs will track the
review and report any FDA correspondence. No EU filing exists yet for the Lyric 350.
