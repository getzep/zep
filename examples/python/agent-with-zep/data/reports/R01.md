Complaint summary R01, dated 2026-02-03, prepared by Priya Raman.

Pemberline Medical received three complaints from Northgate Regional Hospital in
Ireland. The hospital reported that Aster 410 infusion pumps delivered less fluid
than the programmed rate. In one case a patient received a delayed antibiotic dose;
there was no lasting harm to the patient.

A complaint file was opened as QI-1041. The issue was assigned severity high because
under-delivery of therapy is a patient-safety concern. The affected pumps remain in
service at the hospital while the investigation proceeds. The quality team will
request the return of two units for bench inspection at the Galway plant.
