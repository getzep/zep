Risk review R12, dated 2026-07-02, prepared by Marcus Feld.

Engineering tests show that BP-3 battery pack swelling occurs only after more than
400 charge cycles, and only in packs from Carrow Energy lot CE-77.

QI-1057 severity was reclassified from high to medium based on this data.
CAPA-124 was opened; I am the owner. The corrective action is to replace CE-77
packs at the next preventive maintenance visit. Packs from other lots do not show
the swelling failure mode within the tested range.
