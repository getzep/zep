Complaint summary R08, dated 2026-05-20, prepared by Marcus Feld.

Harlow Children's Hospital in the United States reported swollen BP-3 battery
packs in two Aster 520 syringe pumps. The swelling was found during routine
equipment checks. There was no injury to any patient or staff member.

A complaint file was opened as QI-1057. The initial severity was assigned high
because a battery defect can present a safety hazard. The affected packs were
returned to Pemberline for engineering analysis under the complaint file.
