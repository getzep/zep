Field service report R10, dated 2026-06-18, prepared by Colm Brennan.

At St. Brigid's Hospital in Ireland, two Aster 410 pumps running firmware 2.3.1
under-delivered by 6% to 9% on the bench. Both pumps were in rooms at about 16 °C
at the time of the failures.

Under-delivery recurred after the firmware 2.3.1 update. The issue is NOT resolved.
Ambient temperature appears to be a contributing factor: the earlier bench test of
firmware 2.3.1 at 25 °C did not cover this condition. I recommended the quality
team reopen the verification assessment for CAPA-118.
